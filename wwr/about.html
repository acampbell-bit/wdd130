Whitewater Adventures

Hi! My name is Brian Campbell. I am Mark Campbell's great-grandson, and I have been riding this river since before I could remember. I love this work and I love providing a fun and exhilarating experience for anyone who loves nature and wishes to feel the thrill of conquering the river. Come join me and my family on this amazing Whitewater Adventure.

History

Whitewater Adventure began as a passion of our father, grandfather and great-grandfather Mark Campbell in the 1940's. Mark Campbell was said to be a "fish out of water" if he wasn't on the river. He worked and saved until he had enough money to buy his first raft and camping supplies.

His first customer was his older brother and his family. Word quickly spread and soon he was taking church groups, and work groups out on the river. As his family grew his buisness also grew. He soon had sons and daughters who became excellent guides and the buisness has become a family affair.
